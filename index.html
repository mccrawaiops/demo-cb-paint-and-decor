About

CB Paint & Decor, Inc.

Born and raised in Colorado’s Roaring Fork Valley near Aspen, Chris developed his passion for painting and design at an early age. While in high school, he began building his painting skills working on custom homes in the Aspen area. After attending drafting school in Denver, Chris moved to California with hopes of painting sets in Hollywood. When that path didn’t quite work out, he made his way to Santa Barbara in 1987.

Chris became a licensed painting contractor in 1991, and shortly thereafter, CB Paint & Decor, Inc. was established.

With an appreciation for both classic and contemporary styles, Chris brings a creative eye and a love of decorative finishes to his work. His specialty work includes faux finishes, Old World patinas, frescos, stenciling, and other custom decorative techniques that can add character, depth, and individuality to a space. His work can be seen throughout Santa Barbara, from local restaurants and businesses to beautiful homes and estates.

Chris enjoys finding the right colors, textures, finishes, and details to bring each space to life while reflecting the personality and tastes of his clients. Whether creating a timeless Old World look, adding the character of a custom faux finish, or simply giving a room a fresh new feel, he approaches each project with the same creative attention to detail.

Services

How we can help

Interior Painting

Custom wall, ceiling, accent wall, and trim painting executed with premium low-VOC paints for a clean, healthier indoor environment.

Exterior Painting

Weather-resistant coatings designed specifically to withstand Santa Barbara’s unique coastal elements, sun exposure, and marine atmosphere.

Decorative & Specialty Finishes

Artistic finishes that bring character and depth to a space, including faux finishes, Old World patinas, frescos, stenciling, and custom decorative treatments designed to create a truly distinctive look.

Cabinet Refinishing & Repainting

Factory-smooth spray finishes for kitchen, bathroom, and built-in cabinetry.

Drywall & Surface Repair

Seamless texture matching, hole patching, crack repair, and sealing to establish a flawless foundation for paint.

New Construction & Remodel Painting

Professional finishing for custom home builds, room additions, and whole-home renovations, coordinated smoothly with general contractors.

Outdoor Structure Protection

Staining, painting, and sealing for decks, pergolas, gazebos, fascia, and patio covers.

Color Consultations

Hands-on assistance selecting complementary palettes, sheens, and custom color matches tailored to your architecture and natural lighting.

Custom Wallpaper Installation and Removal

Professionally installed wallpaper offers a smooth application, adding individual character to a single wall or whole room.
